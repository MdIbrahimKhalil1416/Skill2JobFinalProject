Our mission

Bridging the gap between learning and hiring

Most job boards stop at "apply." Skill2Job starts one step earlier: it shows a student exactly which skills stand between them and a specific job, and exactly what to learn next to close that gap - for free, wherever possible.

Live counts from this installation's own database, not marketing numbers.

What Skill2Job actually does

%

Weighted skill-match score

Every job lists required skills with a minimum level and an importance weight. Your match is calculated the same transparent way every time - no black box.

+

Course suggestions, free first

Missing or weak skills come with real course links, ranked by priority (importance × gap), free options shown before paid ones.

✓

Portfolio & endorsements

GitHub/LinkedIn/portfolio links, course-progress tracking, and peer or admin skill endorsements - evidence beyond a self-reported level.

⇄

A full hiring workflow

Applicant filtering, bulk shortlist/reject, interview scheduling and in-app chat - employers don't have to leave the platform to run a hiring round.

◷

Never misses a deadline

Bookmark a job and get an in-app and email reminder before its deadline, instead of relying on remembering to check back.

◎

Built on real numbers

Hiring-funnel analytics show employers their actual Applied → Shortlisted → Hired conversion, not a vanity metric.
